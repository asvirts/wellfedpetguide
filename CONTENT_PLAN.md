The diarrhea and peeing H1s match how owners ask the question. Article 6 was checked against the live search results on 3 November 2026 before it was drafted. Google's results for `dog food for picky eaters` are a commercial-investigation query. The pages that rank are format roundups (dry, wet, fresh, toppers) from Chewy, Dog Food Advisor, and Vetstreet, brand collection pages, and veterinary explainers from VCA and the NC State veterinary hospital. That matches an affiliate roundup, so the draft shipped. The angle those roundups skip is the one in the article: separate a healthy choosy dog from a dog who stopped eating, keep toppers at or under 10 percent of calories, and do not treat a grain-free or performance kibble as the default upgrade. Nothing in the queue was skipped. The planner's closest head terms are in the "also target" column and are used in the body.

## Rules for every new draft

- Answer the search. No keyword stuffing, no "we tested" scores, no made-up vet credentials.
- Visible updated date, sources (AVMA, AAFCO, FDA, WSAVA, veterinary schools, Merck where they apply), affiliate disclosure, not-veterinary-advice note.
- Product cards stay TODO until Andrew checks the current bag and listing.
- Roundups (`kind: roundup`) get ItemList schema. No Product or Review star schema.
