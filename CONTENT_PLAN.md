Primary keyword for a draft is the phrase in that article's `primaryKeyword` frontmatter. The H1 contains it. `seoTitle` is what becomes the `<title>` before ` | WellFed Pet Guide`.

The diarrhea and peeing H1s match how owners ask the question. Article 6 was checked against the live search results on 3 November 2026 before it was drafted. Google's results for `dog food for picky eaters` are a commercial-investigation query. The pages that rank are format roundups (dry, wet, fresh, toppers) from Chewy, Dog Food Advisor, and Vetstreet, brand collection pages, and veterinary explainers from VCA and the NC State veterinary hospital. That matches an affiliate roundup, so the draft shipped. The angle those roundups skip is the one in the article: separate a healthy choosy dog from a dog who stopped eating, keep toppers at or under 10 percent of calories, and do not treat a grain-free or performance kibble as the default upgrade.

Article 7 was checked against Google's US results on 5 November 2026 before it was drafted. The query `best dog food for sensitive stomach` is commercial investigation. The page leads with a shopping unit and an AI product list (Hill's Science Diet Sensitive Stomach & Skin, Purina Pro Plan Sensitive Skin & Stomach salmon and rice, Royal Canin Digestive Care), then brand and retailer pages (The Honest Kitchen, Petco vet Q&A, Royal Canin) and forums. The angle those product cards skip: a sensitive stomach is not a diagnosis or an AAFCO category, an over-the-counter limited-ingredient or grain-free bag is not an elimination diet, and an acute bout stays on the diarrhea guide. Secondary phrases used in the draft, besides the planner term `dog food for sensitive stomach`, are those People Also Ask and related-search questions. Nothing in the queue was skipped. The planner's closest head terms are in the "also target" column and are used in the body.

Article 8 was checked on 10 November 2026 before it was drafted. The query `best dog food for allergies` is commercial investigation. Secondary phrases used in the draft, besides the planner terms `dog food for allergies` and `hypoallergenic dog food`, are hydrolyzed dog food, limited-ingredient dog food, novel protein dog food, dog food for itchy skin, and those trial questions. ## Rules for every new draft

- Answer the search. No keyword stuffing, no "we tested" scores, no made-up vet credentials.
- Visible updated date, sources (AVMA, AAFCO, FDA, WSAVA, veterinary schools, Merck where they apply), affiliate disclosure, not-veterinary-advice note.
- Product cards stay TODO until Andrew checks the current bag and listing.
- Roundups (`kind: roundup`) get ItemList schema. No Product or Review star schema.
